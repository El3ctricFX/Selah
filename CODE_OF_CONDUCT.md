# Selah Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Selah is built to be a calm, welcoming place to write, reflect, and grow. We want the project community to be respectful, constructive, and open to people with different backgrounds, beliefs, identities, and levels of experience.

Everyone who participates in Selah's issues, discussions, pull requests, or other project spaces is expected to help make them a positive environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent where reasonable, and ask clarifying questions before jumping to conclusions.
- Give feedback about ideas and code without attacking the people behind them.
- Welcome beginners and explain technical decisions where helpful.
- Respect different backgrounds and beliefs. Faith-related discussion should remain relevant and considerate, not a reason to pressure, demean, or exclude others.
- Respect privacy. Do not share another person's private information, journal entries, prayer requests, or other sensitive content without permission.
- Accept that maintainers may decline or defer ideas to keep the project focused, safe, and maintainable.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable include:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Insults, demeaning comments, or repeated hostile arguments.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or other inappropriate conduct.
- Publishing private information, private messages, or personal journal content without consent.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or repeatedly ignoring reasonable requests from maintainers.
- Using religious belief, lack of belief, identity, background, disability, or experience level as a basis for hostility or exclusion.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in the Selah repository and in project spaces where someone is representing Selah or participating on its behalf, including issues, discussions, pull requests, and community channels.

## Reporting a concern

Please report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the [El3ctricFX GitHub profile](https://github.com/El3ctricFX). Do not include sensitive details in a public issue or discussion.

If you cannot find a private way to contact the maintainer, you may open an issue that simply asks for a private contact route. Do not name the people involved or describe the incident in that public issue.

Reports will be reviewed as carefully as the maintainer's capacity allows. The maintainer will handle reports as respectfully and confidentially as practical, and may remove content, limit participation, or take other action appropriate to the situation. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Questions

If you have questions about this Code of Conduct, open a discussion or contact the maintainer privately.
